What we look at

HTTPS connection, selected response headers, cookie flags, possible HTTP resources, and the availability of robots.txt and security.txt.

⤴

What we don’t do

No crawling, port scanning, vulnerability exploitation, credential checks, or fetching of page assets. Referenced resources are only noted.

i

How to read your results

Missing signals are prompts for review, not proof of a vulnerability.

Each applicable check has a simple weight. The summary is the weighted share marked present among checks that completed. Unknown and not-applicable checks do not count as failures. The score is a narrow snapshot, not an overall security rating.

Read limitations ↓

Limitations & acceptable use

This checker reviews one homepage response and two known public paths. Results can vary by region, time, browser, and site configuration. It may miss controls implemented elsewhere and cannot validate a complete security program. Use it only for systems you own or are authorized to assess; stop if you are unsure about permission.
